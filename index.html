SECTION 4.2 • EXAMPLE 4

Finding the Line of Best Fit for Linearly Related Data

The problem: The table shows 2017 National League baseball teams' on-base percentage, x, and runs scored, y. Use these data to answer all four parts of Example 4.

Questions

(a) Use a graphing utility to find the line of best fit that models the relation between on-base percentage and runs scored.

(b) Graph the line of best fit on the scatter plot obtained in the first example.

(c) Interpret the slope.

(d) Use the line of best fit to predict the number of runs a team will score if its on-base percentage is 32.2.
